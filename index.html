03 / PHILOSOPHY • STUDIO ETHOS

DESIGN IS NOT DECORATION. IT IS AN OPERATING SYSTEM FOR VISUAL CULTURE.

I operate at the intersection of rigorous typography, tactile physical craft, and fluid computation. Inspired by industrial hardware aesthetics, retro dot-matrix interfaces, and playful spring physics, I help progressive cultural institutions and pioneering technology brands establish timeless, unmistakable identities.

Whether engineering an elastic design system for global outdoor billboards or hand-binding a limited edition risograph monograph, every piece is calibrated with intentional weight, motion, and tactile grain.
